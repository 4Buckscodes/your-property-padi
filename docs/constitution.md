# Your Property Padi Constitution

Version: 0.1

---

# Purpose

Your Property Padi exists to help people make confident property decisions through trusted knowledge, intelligent technology, and qualified human expertise.

Our success is measured by better decisions, not by the number of properties sold.

---

# Mission

Reduce uncertainty in every property decision.

---

# Vision

Become Africa's most trusted property intelligence platform.

---

# Core Beliefs

## 1. Trust comes before growth.

We will never sacrifice trust for short-term revenue.

---

## 2. Education comes before recommendation.

Users deserve to understand why something is recommended.

---

## 3. Honesty beats certainty.

If we don't know, we say we don't know.

If information cannot be verified, we clearly state that.

---

## 4. AI assists people.

AI supports human decision-making.

It does not replace qualified legal, financial, surveying, or engineering professionals.

---

## 5. Simplicity wins.

Every explanation should be understandable by someone buying property for the first time.

---

## 6. Every recommendation must have reasoning.

The platform should explain why.

Never provide unexplained recommendations.

---

## 7. Users own their decisions.

We provide information and guidance.

Users remain responsible for their final decisions.

---

# Product Principles

Every feature must answer one question:

Does this reduce uncertainty?

If not, it should not be built.

---

# AI Principles

The AI should always:

Educate

Explain

Ask questions

Identify risks

Recommend actions

Escalate to experts when necessary

Never pretend certainty.

---

# Human Principles

Every user should feel:

Respected

Informed

Protected

Never pressured.

---

# Business Principles

Trust over sales.

Relationships over transactions.

Long-term reputation over short-term profit.

---

# Decision Framework

When facing difficult decisions, ask:

Does this help users make better property decisions?

If yes, proceed.

If no, reconsider.

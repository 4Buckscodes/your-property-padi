# Your Property Padi
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Version: 1.0

Author: Jafar Olawale

Status: Draft

---

# Executive Summary

Your Property Padi is an independent Property Intelligence Platform that helps people make confident property decisions.

The platform combines trusted knowledge, intelligent recommendations and access to verified professionals.

Unlike traditional property portals, Your Property Padi is designed to reduce uncertainty rather than simply advertise properties.

---

# Problem Statement

Buying, selling, renting and investing in property is difficult because people struggle with:

• Lack of trustworthy information

• Property fraud

• Complex documentation

• Scattered market information

• Lack of expert guidance

• Poor investment decisions

---

# Mission

Reduce uncertainty in every property decision.

---

# Vision

Become Africa's most trusted property intelligence platform.

---

# Success Metrics

Users should be able to:

✓ Understand property documents

✓ Compare investment opportunities

✓ Analyze property questions

✓ Learn before buying

✓ Connect with professionals

✓ Make more informed decisions

---

# Version 1 Scope

The first version will focus on education and guidance.

Included:

• Property Intelligence Advisor

• Knowledge Centre

• Property Documents Guide

• Area Guides

• Property Glossary

• FAQs

• Investment Guidance

• Human Consultation

Not Included:

• Property Listings

• Payments

• Property Auctions

• Tenant Management

• Mortgage Marketplace

• Property Insurance

• Construction Management
